# Water Wise Family

Create an app where it suggest the amount of water needed to be saved segregated on based on use of water like how many for drinking, washing, etc. based on your family composition (# of members, age, pregnant , etc.), 



This app is for water scarcity readiness caused by different natural climate patterns like El Nino. 



The app also shows upcoming risk or other factors that could affect water scarcity.
